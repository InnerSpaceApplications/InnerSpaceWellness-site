The Six Domains of Wellness

Inner Space organizes life stressors into six key areas. We’ll help you identify where challenges exist and build a plan that leads to an increased sense of balance and ease.

Physiological

Physical health factors such as sleep, nutrition, and exercise directly influence emotional stability.

Interpersonal

The quality of our relationships shapes how supported or strained we feel in daily life.

Thought Patterns

The stories we tell ourselves color every experience and can either uplift or weigh us down.

Acceptance & Grief

Processing loss and difficult changes allows us to make peace with reality and move forward.

Environmental

Our surroundings can either bring calm and joy or add to stress and discontent.

Hobbies & Routine

Maintaining a healthy balance between responsibilities and enjoyable activities fosters peace of mind.
